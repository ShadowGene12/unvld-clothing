## 2024-03-24 - Massive Resource Drain from Hidden Autoplay Videos
**Learning:** In the `ProductCard` component, placeholder videos for cinematic hover states were rendered with the `autoPlay` attribute. Because there are many products in a grid (like on the `/shop` page), this caused the browser to download and play all of these videos simultaneously on page load, even though they were visually hidden (`opacity-0`) until hovered. This creates a massive bandwidth and CPU bottleneck, especially on mobile devices or slow connections, significantly degrading performance.
**Action:** Always use `preload="none"` and remove `autoPlay` for videos intended for hover states. Programmatically trigger `.play()` and `.pause()` on `onMouseEnter` and `onMouseLeave` via a `useRef` to ensure resources are only consumed when the user actually interacts with the element.
