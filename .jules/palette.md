## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility improvements to hover-based dropdown **Learning:** Found a hover-based Worlds dropdown component in the header that relied exclusively on `onMouseEnter` and `onMouseLeave` on the wrapper, without an `onClick` toggle. This was inaccessible for touch users and lacked keyboard focus accessibility. **Action:** Adding an `onClick` handler with `e.preventDefault()`, an `onFocus` handler, and an `onBlur` handler to properly handle keyboard and touch interactions, alongside explicit `focus-visible` styles and ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-haspopup`, `aria-controls`).
