## 2024-05-24 - Hover Dropdown Keyboard Accessibility
**Learning:** Hover-based dropdown menus that rely solely on `onMouseEnter` and `onMouseLeave` are completely inaccessible to keyboard-only users. Additionally, when implementing an `onBlur` handler to close the dropdown when focus moves outside, checking `!e.currentTarget.contains(e.relatedTarget)` is crucial to distinguish between focus moving entirely outside the component versus just moving between its internal interactive elements (like the toggle button and its dropdown links).
**Action:** When adding accessibility to custom hover-based components, always implement an `onClick` toggle mechanism alongside explicit `focus-visible` styles and appropriate ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-haspopup`). Use the `!e.currentTarget.contains(e.relatedTarget)` check within the container's `onBlur` event to properly close the dropdown only when focus truly leaves the component.
