## 2024-03-20 - Adding Accessibility to Hover-Based Dropdowns
**Learning:** When adding accessibility to custom hover-based components (like dropdown menus) that previously relied solely on mouse events (`onMouseEnter`/`onMouseLeave`), we must implement an `onClick` toggle mechanism alongside explicit `focus-visible` styles and appropriate ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-haspopup`, `aria-controls`). Furthermore, when implementing `onBlur` handlers to close React dropdown menus, use `if (!e.currentTarget.contains(e.relatedTarget))` to correctly determine when focus has moved entirely outside the component, rather than just between its internal elements.
**Action:** Consistently apply these complete accessibility patterns to all hover-based interactive elements, ensuring they are fully usable by keyboard and screen-reader users, rather than relying solely on visual hover states.
