## 2024-05-24 - Accessible Dropdown Menus **Learning:** Hover-based dropdowns lacking native focus and keyboard handlers completely block screen reader and keyboard users from accessing sub-menus, while touch users experience frustrating instant-close behaviors due to simulated mouseenter events. **Action:** Always implement onClick with e.preventDefault() for touch, onFocus, onBlur, and proper ARIA attributes on custom interactive dropdowns instead of relying solely on mouse events.
